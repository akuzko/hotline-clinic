# Hotline Clinic: Product Requirement

> Lesson 02 (Description), Step 1. One paragraph, where every adjective is a decision.

Hotline Clinic lets a person with an urgent but non-emergency problem (an injury, a sick child, or feeling unwell) find the **earliest bookable time** with the right specialist (traumatologist, pediatrician or general practitioner) at one community clinic, and **secure it before leaving home**. "Fast" means the nearest slot is visible within **two taps** and **5 seconds on a Slow 3G phone**, and an anonymous booking takes **under 90 seconds** including SMS confirmation. "Simple" means:

- **no app install and no account**, only a name and phone number
- **three situation buttons** in plain words instead of specialist titles
- a **6-character confirmation code** that is easy to read out at reception

"Real-time" means every viewer sees a slot disappear or reappear **within 2 seconds** of it being held, booked, cancelled, expired or released as a no-show, **without polling**.

- A slot is held the moment a booking starts and released if it isn't confirmed **within 3 minutes**.
- A booked patient who hasn't checked in **15 minutes** after the start time frees the slot.
- A slot already in progress can still be booked inside a configurable late-booking window.

Booking is limited to **today and tomorrow**. The schedule starts at **45-minute appointments plus a 15-minute break**, and all of these values can be changed by an administrator behind a password. Patients with an optional account (name and phone verified by SMS, with an optional email that is confirmed separately) book in one tap and see their earlier anonymous bookings. The product **always shows the emergency number first**, never stores symptoms or medical details, and never allows two patients in the same slot.
